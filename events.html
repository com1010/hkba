HKBA × MEAACBA 簽署歷史性諒解備忘錄（2024年4月17日）

HONG KONG, April 17, 2024 — The Hong Kong Blockchain Association (HKBA.club) is thrilled to announce the signing of a historic Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with the Middle East Africa and Asia Crypto and Blockchain Association (MEAACBA.org). This pioneering initiative underpins a mutual commitment to promote, develop, and encourage blockchain and cryptocurrency technologies across Asia, the Middle East, and Africa.

Under the MOU, HKBA and MEAACBA will embark on a series of joint activities, including research and development initiatives, educational and training programs, conferences and seminars, regulatory advocacy, and public awareness campaigns.

"The partnership with MEAACBA underpins our unwavering commitment to promoting blockchain technology and digital currencies to create a more transparent, secure, and inclusive financial system," said Mr. Tony Tong, Co-Chairman of HKBA.

Echoing these sentiments, Mr. Jehanzeb Awan, Chairman of MEAACBA, added: "This MOU represents a significant milestone in the advancement of blockchain and cryptocurrency technologies across Middle East, Africa, and Asia."
