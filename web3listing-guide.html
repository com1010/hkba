Coinbase Listing Guideline: How to List Your Assets on Coinbase

Source: Coinbase Blog — "Listing assets on Coinbase is free, and always has been" (May 31, 2022)

Coinbase's goal is to provide a neutral marketplace where customers can engage with every safe and compliant crypto asset. Listing an asset on Coinbase is, and has always been, free. Coinbase does not charge listing or application fees to asset issuers, nor prerequisite asset marketing fees.

How the asset listing process works

  1. Apply. Asset issuers start by creating an account in Coinbase Asset Hub and submitting an application with all relevant information about their asset.
  2. Evaluate. Coinbase's Digital Asset Listing Group (DALG) evaluates the information using a standard listing evaluation framework — legal, compliance, and technical security reviews.
  3. List. Once approved by DALG and integrated with the platform, the asset can be listed on the exchange. Being listed does not mean Coinbase endorses the asset.
  4. Monitor. Coinbase constantly monitors all assets and may de-list assets that stop meeting requirements.

Coinbase Earn

Once an asset is listed, issuers may launch educational campaigns through Coinbase Earn: Inquire → Design → Launch. Earn campaigns are passive educational tools, and whether or not an Earn campaign is expected has no impact on listing approval.
